## Loay Motawie — Privacy Policy

Effective: [DATE]

[LEGAL NAME] ("we") runs the Loay Motawie app and the website loaymotawie.com.
This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

### What we collect

**Information you give us**

- Your name, email address and phone number, and your parent's phone number
- Your school and grade
- A profile photo, if you add one
- Homework you hand in: text, photos and files

**Information collected as you use the app**

- Your progress in lesson videos: where you stopped, how long you watched, the
  playback speed, and whether the app was open on your screen. We use this to
  let you pick up where you left off, and to show your progress to you and your
  teacher.
- If you take a screenshot or screen recording while a lesson is showing: a
  record of that, with the lesson and the time. [Delete this item if the feature
  is not switched on when you publish.]
- A notification ID for your device, with its model, operating system version
  and the app version, so we can send you notifications.

**Shown on your screen**

Your name, phone number and account ID are displayed as a watermark over lesson
videos, to discourage copying. The watermark is drawn by the app on your device;
it is not added to the videos themselves.

We do not collect your location or your contacts, we do not show ads, and we do
not use your information to track you across other apps or websites.

### How we use it

To run your account; to give you your lessons, homework and grades; to send the
notifications you have allowed; to let your teacher see your progress; and to
protect lesson content from being copied.

### Who we share it with

- **Your teacher** sees your homework, grades and progress.
- **Your parent**, if their phone number is linked to your account, can see your
  progress.
- **OneSignal** sends push notifications for us. It receives your account ID,
  your device's notification ID and basic information about your device.
- **[Hosting provider]** stores our data for us.

We do not sell your information.

### Keeping it and deleting it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account
at any time in the app, under **Profile → Delete account**, or at
[https://loaymotawie.com/delete-account]. When you do, we delete your personal
information [immediately / within N days]. [If any academic records are kept
after deletion, say which ones, why, and for how long.]

### Students under 18

The app is for secondary-school students. It is not meant for children under 13,
and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you are under 18,
please use the app with your parent's or guardian's knowledge.

### Security

Everything the app sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
